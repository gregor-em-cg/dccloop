This is a narrow specification revision, approved for local mocks only. Normative choices below are policy choices, not measured quality thresholds. [ESTIMATE]

1. **C1: completion and ownership.** Work-order acceptance uses the current store revision (CAS). Result applicability uses the immutable intent, candidate, bundle, profile, scope, cancellation and owner epoch; a changed revision alone does not invalidate a job. Re-read and retry only the transaction on CAS conflict. A replacement controller must verify the previous controller is dead, acquire a new epoch, and explicitly transfer each known active job. A worker keeps its launch epoch; a recorded transfer permits its original receipt to be adopted by the new owner. Obsolete owners cannot commit. Unknown dispatch outcomes remain blocked. [ESTIMATE]
2. **C2: review closure.** Freeze a nonempty set of required review job IDs before dispatch; default one. Each first impression is immutable. All required jobs must provide valid substantive verdicts before V increments, once. Disagreement closes/counts the round once but leaves S and the previous comparable failure set unchanged pending explicit adjudication. Agreeing failures use their common required-failure set; any required-criterion disagreement blocks. Evidence/execution failures do not close a substantive round. Retries retain the same logical reviewer and consume their own attempts. Exploratory reviews require explicit scope and never change acceptance or human decisions. No late reviewer can be silently added to a closed plan. [ESTIMATE]
3. **C3: packaging.** Plan native asset/path preparation before final candidate sealing and proof capture. Normal G4 archives/validates exact reviewed bytes. A package-only repair consumes E plus a job but no R/D and repeats G4. A necessary native rewrite reserves a new R before dispatch, consumes E plus a job, invalidates G1–G5, and requires fresh construction audit, proofs, review and signoff. D is additionally consumed only when a declared creative recipe changes; unknown creative scope blocks. This explicitly revises T20's blanket “new R/D” wording: a declared byte-only native repair has new R but D=0. At R=10, deny such work, preserve the current artifacts, and report budget_exhausted. No semantic-equivalence waiver. [ESTIMATE]
4. **C4: limits and stalls.** `policies/stage1-v1.json` contains the exact finite synthetic TEST_PLAN limits. Reservations count toward caps even before confirmation; starts are separate. The first valid failed review seeds S=1; strict reduction of required failures resets S=0; overlapping unresolved failures increment S; a disjoint failure set starts S=1. This measures criterion resolution only. Missing evidence, tool failures and disagreement preserve S. Two stalls require a different causal hypothesis and rejected-method record. A reset keeps global counters and budgets; a second pair for the same blocker blocks. When clay fails then corrective clay passes, D=2: material must close that cycle and reserve another R (or stop at the cap), retaining the usable clay. [ESTIMATE]
